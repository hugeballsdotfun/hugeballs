Coins launched through Balls are created by third parties and are not endorsed, reviewed or guaranteed by Balls. A founder bond is a public signal that a founder put their own collateral behind a market-cap target — it is not a guarantee of price, liquidity or behaviour. Bond collateral is held by an on-chain escrow program. The program is new and unaudited, and its upgrade authority is currently held by Balls. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
