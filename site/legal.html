2. How bonds work, and what you are trusting

A founder bond is SOL, chosen by the founder, that they lock behind a market-cap target for their coin. In the same transaction that creates the coin on pump.fun, the SOL moves into a vault owned by the Balls escrow program. Only the wallet that pump.fun recorded as the coin's creator can post a bond for it. The bond carries a market-cap target and a 48-hour deadline.

The coin's market cap is read by the contract directly from pump.fun's on-chain bonding-curve account. If it is at or above the target (or the coin has graduated) when the founder claims, the founder receives the bond back. If the deadline passes and the target is not met, the contract uses the bond to buy the coin on pump.fun and burns every token bought; the founder loses the bond. If an expired, unmet bond is not burned within 7 days of its deadline, the founder may reclaim it.

What you are still trusting. The contract has no instruction that lets Balls withdraw a bond. However: (a) the contract is new and has not been independently audited; (b) the contract is upgradeable, and the upgrade key is currently held by Balls, so a future version could in principle behave differently from the one described here; (c) the burn depends on pump.fun's program continuing to work as it does today, and only the Balls keeper and admin wallets can trigger it; and (d) a bond is a signal, not a promise, and does not guarantee a coin's price, liquidity or anyone's future behaviour. A founder can reach a target with their own funds.

Balls intends to reduce these assumptions over time (for example by moving the upgrade key to a multisig and later renouncing it). Bonds already posted stay governed by the contract they were posted to.

3. Risk Disclosure

Digital assets are highly volatile. A coin's price on a bonding curve can move sharply on small trades, and can fall to near zero at any time. You can lose the entire amount you put in, including any bond, which is lost by design if the target is missed. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything.

The escrow contract is unaudited software. Bugs, exploits, a malicious upgrade, or a change to pump.fun's program could cause bond funds to be lost or locked. Do not post a bond you cannot afford to lose.

You are responsible for your own wallet security. Balls never asks for a seed phrase or private key, and no legitimate Balls page ever will. Verify you are on the correct domain before connecting a wallet or signing a transaction.

4. Privacy

Balls does not require an account, an email address, or any personal information. Connecting a wallet shares only your public wallet address, which is already public on the Solana blockchain.

Every launch, bond, claim and burn is a transaction on the public Solana ledger — visible to anyone, forever, independent of Balls. Coin metadata and images you upload (name, symbol, description, image) are stored on Arweave via Irys, uploaded directly from your own wallet — Balls' own servers never see or hold them.

Balls does not use tracking cookies or sell any data, because it does not collect any.
